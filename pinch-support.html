How to play

100,000 hungry sharks circle Pinch's rock. Tap a shark to throw Pinch's toothpick. Your army of crabs throws spears too. Every bite eats a crab; when the army is gone, the next bite ends the run. Tap the floating bubbles for power-ups: Ink cloud (slows sharks), Tidal push (pushes the circle back), Frenzy (automatic throws), Shell guard (blocks bites) and Rally (+3 crabs).

Pearls

Pearls are earned by playing: every shark, combos, missions, the daily dive and rank promotions, plus optional videos. Spend them in the Armory on upgrades and skins, or before a run on bubble boosts and extras. Pearls cannot be bought with money.

Daily dive, missions and ranks

Claim the daily dive once a day to grow your 7-day streak (missing a day restarts it). Three new missions arrive every day at local midnight. Your rank rises with every shark you have ever repelled.

Ads

Banners appear on menus only, never during a run. A full-screen ad may play between runs. Videos for rewards are always your choice. Change ad privacy choices from the home screen where available, and tracking permission in iOS Settings.
