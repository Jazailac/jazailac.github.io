The rules

Place exactly one crown in every row, column and colored region. Crowns cannot touch, even diagonally. Distant diagonal crowns are allowed.

Controls

In Crown mode, tap a cell to place or remove a crown. In Mark mode, tap or drag to mark empty cells. Dots show cells ruled out by your crowns. Undo reverses a move.

Coins, hints and checks

Coins pay for help. The first check on each board is included; after that a check costs 5 coins. A hint costs 10 coins, rising by 5 on the same board up to 25. Earn coins by solving new levels (more with no hints), keeping stars in practice lessons, collecting the daily gift, or by choosing to watch a short video. Coins never expire and cannot be bought.

Practice lessons

Each lesson is three rounds of one technique on different boards, with three hearts. A crown that isn't forced, or a cross on a crown's space, costs a heart. Keep every heart and skip hints to earn three stars.

Levels and ads

Finish a level to open the next. Every level can be reached by playing, with occasional ads between levels and never during a board. The one-time upgrade removes ads and opens every level and special mode immediately. It is not a subscription.

Restore a purchase

In Settings, choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that bought the upgrade. An internet connection is required. For payment issues, visit Apple purchase support.

Progress

Progress is saved on this device only. Settings → Reset local progress erases it. Deleting the app removes its local data.
