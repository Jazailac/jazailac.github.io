PINCH privacy policy

PINCH: Hold the Circle ("the app") is a single-player game. Your progress, pearls, upgrades, skins, streak, missions and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a game server or an account system, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or photos.

Your crab's name

If you choose to type an Instagram username for your crab, it is stored only on your device and drawn on your crab's flag and on score images you decide to share. It is never sent to us.

The crab army

The other crabs carry the public Instagram usernames of people who asked to join the army by commenting on @thelastcrab posts. No other information about them is included. To have a username removed from the next app update, email us from that account's owner or message @thelastcrab on Instagram.

Advertising and your choices

The game is free and supported by Google AdMob: banner ads on menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between runs, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to bring your crabs back). Google AdMob may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device identifiers, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.

On first launch the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, the identifier is not available and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form lets you choose your ad privacy preferences, and you can change them later with "Ad privacy choices" on the home screen.

See the Google privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing

The Share button creates a score image on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Only what you choose to send leaves your device, to the destination you pick.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Device backups may include it according to your Apple settings. Deleting the app does not delete data already processed by Google; use Google's privacy tools for that, or email us and we will help.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13.
