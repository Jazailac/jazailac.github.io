Your puzzles. Your device.

Crown Garden: Logic Puzzles ("the app") stores puzzle progress, preferences, lesson counts and your coin balance locally on your device. We do not operate a game server or an account system, and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.

Advertising and your choices

Free play is supported by Google AdMob, which shows interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for coins or a free special-mode play. Google AdMob may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device identifiers, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

We request non-personalized ads, limit ads to content suitable for general audiences, disable the publisher first-party identifier and do not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). Non-personalized advertising still involves data collection by Google. Where required, Google's consent form lets you choose your ad privacy preferences, and you can revisit them in the app under Settings → Ad privacy. Buying the one-time upgrade removes all ads.

For how Google handles and retains advertising data, see the Google privacy policy. Third parties that process data for the app are expected to protect it at least as well as this policy describes.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The app reads verified purchase entitlements to unlock the full library. We never receive payment card details. Coins cannot be bought; they are earned through play, a daily gift and optional videos.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings → Reset local progress in the app to erase saved boards, lessons, coins and achievements, or delete the app to remove its local files. Device backups may include local game data according to your Apple settings. Resetting progress does not delete data already processed by advertising providers; contact Google through its privacy policy for those requests, or email us and we will help.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

Optional sharing

The iOS share sheet sends only the result text you choose to share, to the destination you select.
